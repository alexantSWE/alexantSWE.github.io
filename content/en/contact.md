The quickest way to reach me is [Telegram](https://t.me/ariresa). You can also look through my code, scripts, and small projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/alexantSWE).

To get to a useful answer faster, these three details are enough:

1. What is not working, or what task should become easier?
2. When did it start, and what changed beforehand?
3. What have you already tried?

You do not need the technical name for the problem. An ordinary description, a screenshot of the error, or a few lines of terminal output is enough to begin.
