A few questions that usually come up before we start. If yours is not here, ask in [Contact](/en/contact/).

## Do you work remotely or in person?

Everything is done remotely, from anywhere you have internet and access. It does not matter where in Iran or the world you are.

## Which systems do you support?

Linux, Windows, and Android. Not macOS — I am not well-versed in it, and I would rather decline work I do not know well.

## How is the price decided?

I do not put fixed prices on the site, because every problem is different. You explain it, I tell you what is needed and what it costs; if it is not for you, we stop there.

## Is the first step paid?

No. Looking at the problem and telling you where to start is free. Paid work only begins once we both agree on the scope and the price.

## How long does it take?

It depends on the problem and on what I already have in progress. In the first message I will honestly say when I can start and roughly how long I expect it to take.

## What if you cannot fix it?

If I can see early on that it is not going anywhere, I will say so plainly rather than waste your time. Paid work is always made clear before it begins.

## Is my information kept private?

Yes. This site has no trackers, analytics, or cookies and collects nothing about you. The details of your problem stay with me and are not published anywhere.

## How do we start?

A short message on [Telegram](https://t.me/ariresa) or to {{< email >}}. Just tell me what is not working, when it started, and what you have already tried.
